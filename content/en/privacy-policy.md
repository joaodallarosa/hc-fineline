## Privacy Policy

Helena de Carvalho is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect and use your data.

### Data collected

- **Contact data**: Name, email, phone number shared via WhatsApp for booking purposes.
- **Analytics data**: Anonymous visit statistics (with your consent).

### Use of data

Your personal data is used solely to:
- Handle appointments and communicate with you
- Improve our services

### Your rights

In accordance with GDPR, you have the right to access, correct and delete your data. To exercise these rights, contact us via Instagram or WhatsApp.

### Contact

For any privacy-related questions: contact us via WhatsApp or Instagram.
