---
steps:
  - title: "Days 1–3: the protective film"
    body: "Keep the protective film (Saniderm or clingfilm) in place for the recommended 24 to 72 hours. Avoid removing it before the advised time."
  - title: "First wash"
    body: "Gently remove the film, wash the tattoo with lukewarm water and a mild unscented soap. Rinse thoroughly, pat dry with a clean towel (do not rub)."
  - title: "Daily moisturising"
    body: "Apply a thin layer of healing cream (Bepanthen or similar) 2 to 3 times a day. Avoid scented creams and heavy oils."
  - title: "Weeks 2–4: peeling phase"
    body: "Do not scratch or pick at the skin. Peeling is normal — it's part of the healing process."
  - title: "Sun protection"
    body: "Once healed, always protect your tattoo from the sun with SPF50+. Sun is the main enemy of fineline tattoos."
avoid:
  - "Direct sunlight and UV during healing"
  - "Prolonged baths, swimming pools, sea or jacuzzi"
  - "Scratching, rubbing or picking at the skin"
  - "Tight clothing over the tattooed area"
  - "Scented creams or alcohol-based products on the tattoo"
  - "Intense physical activity in the first few days"
normal:
  - "Slight redness in the first 24–48 hours"
  - "Mild swelling around the tattoo"
  - "Itching during the peeling phase"
  - "Skin peeling and renewing itself"
  - "A slightly dull appearance before full healing"
whenToContact: "Contact me if you notice signs of infection (pus, significant redness, excessive heat) or if you have any doubts about your tattoo's healing."
---
Your tattoo has just been done — it's now time to take good care of it for the best result. Follow these steps for the 2 to 4 weeks of healing.
