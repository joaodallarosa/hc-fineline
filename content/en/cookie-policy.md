## Cookie Policy

Our website uses cookies to improve your browsing experience.

### Necessary cookies

These cookies are essential for the site to function. They include language preferences and cookie consent settings.

### Analytics cookies

With your consent, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This data is anonymous and does not allow personal identification.

### Managing your preferences

You can update your preferences at any time by clicking the cookie management button at the bottom of the page.
