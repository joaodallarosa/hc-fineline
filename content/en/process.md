::content-faq-section
---
title: Frequently asked questions
items:
  - question: "How much does a tattoo cost?"
    answer: "Prices depend on size, complexity and session length. Get in touch with your project for a personalised quote."
  - question: "Is a deposit required?"
    answer: "Yes, a deposit is required to confirm your appointment. It will be deducted from the total price of your tattoo."
  - question: "How long does healing take?"
    answer: "Full healing generally takes 2 to 4 weeks. Avoid sun exposure, prolonged bathing and scented creams during this period."
  - question: "Can I choose my own design?"
    answer: "Absolutely! If you have a precise idea, share it with me. I can also propose designs based on your wishes."
  - question: "Do you do colour tattoos?"
    answer: "I specialise in black and grey fineline tattooing. For subtle colours or botanical colour work, feel free to ask."
  - question: "Which body areas are most painful?"
    answer: "Pain varies by person and area. Bony areas (ribs, ankles, spine) are generally more sensitive. I'll support you throughout the session."
